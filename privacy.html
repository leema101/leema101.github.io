Privacy policy

This policy covers The BibleApp and the other apps listed on this site.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app uses Google Drive only to find its own library folder (My Drive/Projects/BibleApp) and to read and write the files in it: Bible texts you supply, your highlights, notes and tags, and the app's sync files.

Where your data goes

Nowhere else. The app talks only to Google's sign-in and Drive services. It has no server of its own, collects no analytics, shows no ads, and does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.

The sign-in token is stored only on your device, in the app's own private storage, and is never sent anywhere except to Google.

Your control

You can sign out in the app's Settings at any time, and revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the device.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
